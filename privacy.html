Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 20, 2026

At VeriMedia.xyz, privacy is not just a feature—it is our absolute core value. Because this tool is engineered to help you strip invasive location tracking and camera serial numbers, we enforce a strict Zero-Data Collection Policy.

1. 100% Local Browser Processing

VeriMedia operates entirely client-side. When you drop or upload a JPEG image into the sandbox, the file is parsed and modified in under 50ms using local browser APIs.

  • No Uploads: Your images are never sent, uploaded, or transmitted to any external server or backend.
  • Edge Computation: The EXIF purging, verified copyright injection, and AI opt-out tag insertions happen strictly inside your browser's local sandbox memory cache.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

Because all computation is local, we maintain a complete vacuum of personal metadata:

  • We do not store, view, or retain your uploaded photos or their metadata.
  • We do not log coordinates, device models, or timestamp details.
  • We do not maintain user profile databases, track user IPs, or record conversion metrics.

3. Third-Party Billing & License Activation

If you choose to purchase Creator Pro:

  • Transactions: Payments are processed securely via our Merchant of Record partners (e.g., Lemon Squeezy, Paddle, or PayPal). We do not collect or store credit card details or bank routing information.
  • License Keys: When validating a license key, a secure, anonymized HTTPS request is dispatched directly from your browser to the payment processor's verification endpoint. The verified status and instance token are cached locally in your browser's localStorage.

4. Cookies & Analytics

We do not deploy marketing trackers, Google Analytics, or invasive ad cookies.

We utilize standard HTML5 local storage (localStorage) solely to memorize whether your Creator Pro tier is currently activated on your active browser instance. This allows you to close and open the application without re-entering your license credentials.

5. Policy Changes

We will never sell your non-existent data, nor will we ever alter this code to quietly siphon files to external servers. Any structural updates to this tool will remain open-source and visible in our local tool declarations and build trees.

6. Contact Us

For privacy audits or technical queries regarding our EXIF stripping libraries, contact us at privacy@verimedia.xyz.
