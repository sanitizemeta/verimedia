Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 23, 2026

At VeriMedia.xyz, privacy is our core value. This tool is designed to strip invasive location tracking and device identifiers from your files. We enforce a strict Zero-Data Collection Policy.

1. 100% Local Browser Processing

VeriMedia operates entirely client-side. When you drop or upload a JPEG image into the sandbox, the file is parsed and modified in under 50ms using local browser APIs.

  • No Uploads: Your images are never sent, uploaded, or transmitted to any external server or backend.
  • Edge Computation: The EXIF purging, verified copyright injection, and AI opt-out (ai:opt-out=true) tag insertions happen strictly inside your browser's local sandbox memory cache.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

Because all computation is local, we maintain a complete vacuum of personal metadata:

  • We do not store, view, or retain your uploaded photos or their metadata.
  • We do not log coordinates, device models, or timestamp details.
  • We do not maintain user profile databases, track user IPs, or record conversion metrics.

3. Third-Party Billing & License Activation

If you choose to purchase Creator Plus or Creator Pro:

  • Transactions: Payments are processed securely via our Merchant of Record partners (e.g., Paddle). We do not collect or store credit card details or bank routing information.
  • License Keys: When validating a license key, a secure request is dispatched directly from your browser to the payment processor. The status is cached locally.

Image Converter & Compressor (Chrome Extension)

Our Image Converter & Compressor Chrome extension follows the same zero-data-collection model as the website:

  • 100% local: All image conversion, compression, resizing and cropping happen inside your browser. Your images are never uploaded, transmitted, or seen by us.
  • Minimal permissions: The extension requests only the storage permission, used solely to remember your Pro activation flag and default settings on your device. It does not access your browsing history, tabs, or the content of websites.
  • License activation: If you purchase Pro, the extension sends only your license key and a randomly generated, non-identifying device ID to our license server to validate the key. No personal, browsing, or image data is sent.
  • License email: Your license key is emailed to the address you provided to our payment processor (Paddle), delivered via our email provider (Resend), so you can recover your key.

4. Cookies & Analytics

We do not deploy marketing trackers or invasive ad cookies.

We use local storage only to remember your Pro activation status.

Privacy FAQ

Are my files uploaded to VeriMedia servers?

No. The sanitizer runs in your browser and the selected media file stays on your device.

What data is stored for paid plans?

The browser stores your license key and creator profile locally. Payment processing is handled by Paddle.

Can I remove my local profile data?

Yes. Clear the profile fields or clear this site's browser storage to remove locally saved settings.
