Shotglow is installed 🎉

Three things, then you're set.

Shotglow runs 100% on your device. Your screenshots are never uploaded, and there's no account to create. Here's the fastest way in.

01 / PIN IT

Keep it one click away

Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, find Shotglow, and hit the pin. The camera icon is your capture menu.

02 / LEARN 3 KEYS

Capture without the mouse

Alt+Shift+V visible area · Alt+Shift+R select region · Alt+Shift+F full page. Rebind any of them at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

03 / FIRST SHOT

Capture → annotate → copy

Take a capture and the editor opens: add arrows, text, or numbered steps, drag the blur over anything sensitive, pick a gradient frame, then hit Copy to clipboard and paste it anywhere.
