Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I find my Creator Pro license key?

After purchasing, check your email for the Paddle receipt. Your license key is the Transaction ID found in the receipt (it usually starts with txn_). Just copy and paste that into the Activation menu if it didn't activate automatically!

How do I block AI web crawlers from using my images?

VeriMedia embeds a standard "AI Opt-Out" tag (ai:opt-out = true) directly into your image file. This signals to major AI web crawlers (like GPTBot and ClaudeBot) that your work is off-limits for AI model training.

How do I add copyright info and licensable badges to my photos?

By embedding standard creator copyright links directly into the image files, search engines like Google can identify you as the author and display a "Licensable" badge next to your images in search results.

Why should I strip location metadata before uploading photos?

Hidden metadata inside raw photos contains your precise GPS coordinates, camera details, and dates. Stripping this data protects your location privacy and removes identifiers that bots can scrape.

How to Protect Images from AI Scrapers and Remove EXIF Data

VeriMedia provides an advanced, browser-based solution for photographers and digital creators to maintain privacy and ownership. Our primary utility is an EXIF data remover and GPS metadata stripper that ensures no private location tracking remains in your shared media. By physically purging camera serial numbers and device fingerprints, we prevent forensic carving of your private data.

In response to the rise of generative AI, VeriMedia serves as an AI Training Shield. We embed the standardized ai:opt-out=true header directly into the JPEG and PDF binary structures. This signals to ethical AI scrapers like GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and Common Crawl that your work is restricted from model training datasets.

Enabling the Google Licensable Badge

Beyond privacy, VeriMedia enhances your professional presence by enabling the Google Images Licensable Badge. By injecting IPTC Copyright fields and a WebStatement URL into your image metadata, search engines can correctly attribute authorship and provide a direct link to your licensing terms or portfolio.

Secure PDF Scrubbing

Our PDF Forensic Cleaner goes beyond simple property removal. It scans the internal document streams to physically overwrite hidden author names, creation timestamps, and software signatures that standard PDF readers often miss. This ensures your documents are safe for legal, corporate, or sensitive public distribution.
