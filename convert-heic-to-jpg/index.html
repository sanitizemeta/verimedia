Why convert HEIC to JPG?

  • Compatibility: JPG opens on every device, browser, and app. HEIC often does not.
  • Sharing: upload to any website, forum, or form without an "unsupported file" error.
  • Editing: many older photo editors cannot read HEIC.

JPG is a lossy format, but at high quality the difference is invisible to the eye. VeriMedia lets you choose the quality level, so you can balance file size against fidelity. For archiving originals, keep your HEIC files; for sharing and compatibility, a high-quality JPG is ideal.
